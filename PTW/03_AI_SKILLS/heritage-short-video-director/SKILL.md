# Heritage Short Video Director

## Scope and expected output

Input: approved cultural source, topic, intended length, footage access. Output: storyboard and shot list.

## Procedure

1. Extract three defensible facts and one vivid location/craft insight from source editorial data.
2. Write a 2-second hook, visual progression, concise Vietnamese narration and website CTA.
3. Provide beat-by-beat shot, caption and soundtrack direction without copying copyrighted music.
4. Distinguish real footage, illustration and AI-generated reconstruction visually and in caption.
5. List missing filming permissions, model releases and asset rights before publication.
6. Include on-screen source attribution and output dimensions 1080x1920.

## Evidence and quality gate

- Read `references/project-context.md` for project-specific sources.
- Mark each important statement VERIFIED, INFERRED, or UNVERIFIED.
- Never claim to have accessed social analytics or live Readdy backend without evidence.
- Preserve source links for any externally sourced factual assertions.
- Provide result in Vietnamese unless the user requests another language.
- Protect personal data, confidential exports and credentials; do not upload them into social posts, GitHub or public slides.
