# Heritage Social Planner

## Scope and expected output

Input: approved editorial items and goals. Output: cross-channel calendar with CTA and status.

## Procedure

1. Use only the exact official-looking links declared in SocialDock.tsx until account ownership is confirmed.
2. Assign Facebook to community discussion and educational carousel, TikTok to short video discovery.
3. Map each post to a source article/location/story slug and a measurable CTA.
4. Propose publication windows as hypotheses, not performance-proven optimal times.
5. Keep metrics blank unless account analytics or public evidence verifies them.
6. Return a dated calendar, asset checklist, compliance notes, and measurement plan.

## Evidence and quality gate

- Read `references/project-context.md` for project-specific sources.
- Mark each important statement VERIFIED, INFERRED, or UNVERIFIED.
- Never claim to have accessed social analytics or live Readdy backend without evidence.
- Preserve source links for any externally sourced factual assertions.
- Provide result in Vietnamese unless the user requests another language.
- Protect personal data, confidential exports and credentials; do not upload them into social posts, GitHub or public slides.
