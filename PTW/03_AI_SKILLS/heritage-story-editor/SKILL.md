# Heritage Story Editor

## Scope and expected output

Input: existing text, oral-history source or approved notes. Output: edited narrative with evidence and verification notes.

## Procedure

1. Identify whether content is recorded tradition, folklore, interview or historically verified evidence.
2. Maintain the storyteller voice while checking internal consistency of named sites, dates and crafts.
3. Never turn oral legend into established historical fact without independent citations.
4. Structure hook, setting, central human detail, cultural meaning and ethical CTA.
5. Flag consent and attribution requirements for identifiable people and submitted photos.
6. Return both publish-ready copy and a short fact-check register.

## Evidence and quality gate

- Read `references/project-context.md` for project-specific sources.
- Mark each important statement VERIFIED, INFERRED, or UNVERIFIED.
- Never claim to have accessed social analytics or live Readdy backend without evidence.
- Preserve source links for any externally sourced factual assertions.
- Provide result in Vietnamese unless the user requests another language.
- Protect personal data, confidential exports and credentials; do not upload them into social posts, GitHub or public slides.
